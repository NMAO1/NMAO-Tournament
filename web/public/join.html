Why NMAO

A tournament built for students, schools, and teachers, first.

NMAO Tournament League exists to give students across the nation a stage. Every student, in every dojo, at every rank, deserves the chance to compete, to grow, and to be seen. Students compete each month by video from their own dojo, judged by experienced martial artists, building a season-long record of their growth.

This is more than a tournament. It is a community of dojos rising together. Students earn the spotlight, schools share in regular revenue and in the grand-finale cash prizes, and experienced instructors earn well-paying judging positions. We built it for you, first.

How it works

A season, run from your own mat.

01Compete from your own mat

Film one form or weapons video on a phone, up to 2 angles for a clear view. That's the entry. One round a month.

02Fair divisions

Division pods of no more than 15, sorted by age, rank, and rating, reshuffled each round for a fair chance every time.

03Judged with honor

Scored by experienced martial artists, paid well for their craft. Effort is honored as loudly as outcome.

04Duel between rounds

Between tournaments, competitors nationwide face off head to head, and the community votes on who prevails.

05Nine rounds, one journey

Nine monthly rounds lead to the semi-finals and a grand finale. Your best five rounds count, so a month you miss never breaks your season.

The keepsake

Earned one piece at a time.

Every round earns a segment of the season medallion, in the metal your student placed. Nine pieces make the whole.

Badges for the journey

Placing earns medals, but progress earns badges. Every competitor is recognized for milestones, effort, and steady growth, so a student who did not place still leaves with something earned, and every reason to come back stronger.

Questions

What instructors ask first.

What does it cost my school?

Nothing to join. Students pay a small entry per round, and up to 30% of every entry comes back to your school each month.

How much of my time does it take?

Very little. Your students film one video a month from your mat. Judging, scoring, and divisions are handled for you.

What do my students need?

A phone and your mat. One form or weapons video, up to two angles. That's the entry.

What is dueling?

Between tournament rounds, your students face other competitors head to head — one video against another — and the community votes on the winner. It keeps them competing all month long.

Do we have to compete every month?

No. Your best five of nine rounds count, so a missed month never breaks a season, and you can host your own in-house events any time.
