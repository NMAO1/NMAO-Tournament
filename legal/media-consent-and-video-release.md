By signing, I (the competitor, and if the competitor is a minor, the parent/legal
guardian on the minor's behalf) agree to the following.

## 1. What is collected
To participate, NMAO collects: the competitor's **name, date of birth, school/class,
guardian contact** (for minors), and the **performance video(s)** the competitor
submits, plus scores and results generated from them.

## 2. Permission to use the video (release)
I authorize NMAO to **collect, store, and use** the submitted performance video(s)
and the competitor's name, school, and results **for operating the tournament**:
having the video **judged**, generating scores/placements/ratings, showing results
and the competitor's progress within the app, and — subject to the privacy settings
in §4 — on results and standings pages.

## 3. Community "Dueling" — peer viewing & voting (opt-in)
**If dueling is enabled for this competitor by their school**, I understand and agree
that the competitor's duel video(s) **may be shown to, and voted on by, other
verified NMAO competitors** in a **closed, verified community** (never the public
web). This community feature operates with these protections:
- **Verified members only** — no public or anonymous viewers.
- **No free-text comments** — voting is a simple "who won?" with, at most, preset
  encouragements ("Strong stance!").
- **Anonymous vote tallies**; no public discovery, no direct messaging.
- **Sharing is off by default**; the guardian may **disable dueling, voting, and
  notifications** at any time.

I understand this peer-voting permission is **separate and optional**, and that
declining it does not affect the competitor's ability to compete in the main
tournament.

## 4. Privacy & guardian control
- The competitor's video and data are **private by default** and **guardian-
  controlled** for minors.
- NMAO will **not** use the video or the competitor's data for **behavioral
  advertising**, will **not sell** it, and will **not** make a minor **publicly
  discoverable**. Any sponsor content is clearly labeled and non-behavioral.
- I may **review, download, or request deletion** of the competitor's video and
  personal data at any time by [CONTACT / IN-APP CONTROL], and NMAO will delete it
  within [X DAYS] except where retention is required by law.
- NMAO's full **Privacy Policy** at [URL] governs how data is handled.

## 5. Name & likeness
I grant NMAO permission to use the competitor's **name, school, and performance
results** within the app and on results/standings pages, subject to §4. Use of the
competitor's likeness in any **promotional/highlight** context is **guardian-gated
and off by default** for minors, and requires separate opt-in.

## 6. Guardian consent (minors)
