Tournament Privacy Policy

Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("NMAO," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with the NMAO video martial-arts tournament platform, including our mobile and web applications for competitors, parents/guardians, schools, and judges (together, the "Service"). The NMAO Tournament platform is operated separately from, but alongside, the NMAO Accreditation and NMAO Member Platform products.

This is a product used by children. Competitors may be as young as 7. We collect a minor's information — including video of the minor — only with a parent's or legal guardian's verifiable consent, and we handle that information with special care. See Children's Privacy (COPPA) below.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

When a school, a parent/guardian, or an adult competitor creates an account and enters the tournament, we collect:

  • Competitor information: name, date of birth or age bracket, declared rank (beginner / intermediate / advanced), the martial-arts school the competitor is affiliated with, and event entries.
  • Parent/guardian information (for minors): name, relationship to the competitor, email address, and phone number, used to obtain consent and to communicate about the competitor's participation.
  • Competition video: the video each competitor submits for an event, which is the item that is judged.
  • Account and contact details for schools, instructors, and judges who use the Service.
  • Support and correspondence you send to us.

Competition video

Competition videos are hosted with our video subprocessor (Vimeo) as private / unlisted content that is not publicly discoverable. Videos are accessible within the Service to the competitor and their parent/guardian, the judges assigned to score that entry, the competitor's school, and NMAO staff. We describe how long we keep videos, and how they may be used, under Data Retention and Children's Privacy below.

Payment information

Entry fees and related payments are processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not collect or store full payment-card numbers on our servers; that information is handled by Stripe under its own security and privacy practices. We retain limited payment records (such as amount, date, and a payment reference) for accounting, tax, and dispute-resolution purposes.

Shipping information

Collectible medals are shipped in bulk to the competitor's school for the instructor to distribute. We collect the school's shipping address for this purpose; we do not require competitors' home addresses to ship medals.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Service we automatically collect standard technical information such as device and browser type, IP address, app version, and usage and diagnostic logs, used to operate, secure, and improve the Service.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Run the tournament — sort entries into fair divisions and pods, route videos to judges, score entries, calculate placements, ratings, and season standings, and generate medal shipping lists.
  • Process entry-fee payments and calculate and pay school revenue shares.
  • Communicate with competitors, guardians, schools, and judges about rounds, deadlines, results, and account matters.
  • Keep the platform safe — including reviewing reported content and enforcing our rules and code of conduct.
  • Use competition videos and a competitor's likeness for promotional and marketing material, only where a parent/guardian (or an adult competitor) has agreed to this in the applicable consent or waiver, and for no other purpose.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and the rights of others.

How We Share Information

With the competitor's school

A competitor's school can see that competitor's entries, results, ratings, medals, and related information so the school can support the competitor and receive its shipments and revenue share.

With judges

To score an entry, an assigned judge is given access to that entry's video and the information needed to judge it. Judges are excluded from scoring competitors from their own school, and are bound by confidentiality expectations.

We share information with vendors that help us operate the Service, only as needed to perform their functions:

For legal reasons

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety, rights, or property of competitors (including children), the public, or NMAO.

In connection with a business transfer

If NMAO is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use children's information for behavioral advertising.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The Service is designed for use by children as young as 7, and we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We collect personal information from a child — including the child's competition video and the information listed above — only after obtaining verifiable consent from the child's parent or legal guardian. That consent is obtained through the NMAO Parental Consent & Video Release, which the guardian reviews and signs before the child competes.

A parent/guardian may, at any time:

  • review the personal information and videos we have collected about their child;
  • request that we correct or delete their child's personal information and videos; and
  • withdraw consent and stop any further collection or use, which ends the child's participation.

We do not condition a child's participation on disclosing more information than is reasonably necessary. To exercise these rights, contact the child's school or email support@nmao.us; we will verify the parental relationship before acting on the request.

Data Retention

We retain account and competition information for as long as an account is active and as needed to operate the Service, and afterward as needed to comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations and to resolve disputes.

Competition videos. With the consent captured in the Parental Consent & Video Release, competition videos are retained on an ongoing (indefinite) basis so that competitors and their families can look back on their growth over the seasons, and so we may use them for promotional material as described above. Videos are kept private and are used only to operate the tournament and for promotional material — for no other purpose. A parent/guardian (or adult competitor) may request deletion of their videos at any time by emailing support@nmao.us, and we honor such requests. We also delete information collected from anyone who does not complete the required consent.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact your school or email support@nmao.us. We may need to verify your identity, and some information must be retained for legal or operational reasons.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including row-level access controls, encryption in transit, and restricted access to systems and video. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

California and Other U.S. State Residents

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) provides additional rights, including the right to know what categories of personal information we collect, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information and do not engage in cross-context behavioral advertising. Residents of other states with comparable laws may have similar rights. To exercise these rights, contact support@nmao.us.

International Users

The Service is operated in the United States. If you access it from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

Cookies and Tracking

We use only the cookies and similar technologies needed to sign you in and operate the Service. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Changes affecting the collection or use of children's information will not apply retroactively without renewed parental consent where required.
