**What this app is**
NMAO Compete is the competitor app for the National Martial Arts Organization's real-world youth martial-arts league. A parent/guardian registers their child, and the child competes in a real, judged, season-long tournament series: they submit a performance video for each event, certified human judges score it, and results, ratings, and physical awards follow. It is a guardian-managed app for minors — every account is created and consented to by a parent/guardian at onboarding (media/likeness consent, competition rules & code of conduct, and privacy policy).

**Payments are entry into a real-world competition (Guideline 3.1.3(e))**
The only purchases are **entry fees to compete in the real-world tournament** — either a single event entry or a season pass covering the season's tournaments. This is not digital content, in-app currency, or an unlockable app feature. Paying enters the competitor into a live, judge-scored competition that culminates in **physical awards shipped to the athlete** (medals and a collectible medallion) and real-world placement. Because the purchase delivers a real-world service, entry fees are handled through the organization's own web checkout, consistent with Guideline 3.1.3(e) (goods and services used outside the app). There are no digital-only goods sold in the app.

**Safety / user-generated content (Guideline 1.2)**
Competition videos are user-generated. The app includes: a **report** control on every video, the ability to **block** another competitor (managed under Profile → Blocked accounts), a guardian-accepted **zero-tolerance policy** for objectionable content or abusive behavior at onboarding, and removal of offending content and accounts. Minor competitors are shown by first name + last initial only.

**Account deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v))**
An in-app account deletion flow is available under Profile → Delete account, which permanently deletes the account and associated data.

**Privacy**
Privacy Policy is reachable in-app (Profile → Privacy Policy) and at https://school.nmao.us/privacy.html. Guardian consent, data collected about minors, retention, and deletion are described there.

**Suggested walkthrough**
1. Sign in with the demo account above.
2. **Compete tab** — the competitor's home: open round, event status board, and ratings. (Entry purchase is real-world event entry via web checkout — no in-app digital goods.)
3. **Duel / Arena** — watch two performances and vote; try the report/block controls on a video.
4. **Profile** — ratings, honors, Privacy Policy, and the account-deletion flow.

No Apple Pay, no in-app-purchase content, and no third-party/social sign-in are used (first-party email sign-in only).
