Parental Consent & Video Release

Required before a minor competes · Last updated: September 1, 2026

This form is completed by the parent or legal guardian ("Guardian") of a competitor under 18 ("Competitor") before the Competitor takes part in the NMAO video martial-arts tournament operated by NMAO Inc. ("NMAO"). It is our request for verifiable parental consent under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and our video release. Please read it carefully. Our full Tournament Privacy Policy describes how we handle information.

By completing and signing this form, you confirm that you are the Competitor's parent or legal guardian and that you have the authority to give the consents below on the Competitor's behalf.

Consent to Participate

I give permission for my child to enter and take part in NMAO video tournaments, which involves submitting a video of my child performing a martial-arts event (such as forms or weapons) each round for judging.

Consent to Collect My Child's Information

I consent to NMAO collecting and using my child's information as described in the Privacy Policy, including my child's name, age or age bracket, declared rank, school affiliation, event entries, results, rating, and the competition videos my child submits. I understand NMAO collects only the information reasonably needed to run the tournament.

Video Release and Permitted Use

I grant NMAO permission to collect, store, and host my child's competition videos, and to display them within the platform to the judges assigned to score the entry, to my child's school, and to NMAO staff, for the purpose of judging and producing results.

I further grant NMAO permission to use my child's competition videos and likeness in promotional and marketing material for NMAO and its tournaments (for example, highlight clips, social posts, and advertisements).

I understand that:

  • the videos will be stored on an ongoing (indefinite) basis, so my family can look back on my child's progress over time;
  • the videos will be used only to operate the tournament and for promotional material — for no other purpose, and they will never be sold; and
  • this permission is granted without any expectation of payment or royalty.

Privacy and Safety of My Child's Video

I understand that competition videos are hosted privately and are not made publicly discoverable, and that access is limited to my child and me, the judges assigned to that entry, my child's school, and NMAO staff, plus any use in promotional material as described above.

My Rights as Guardian

I understand that at any time I may:

  • review the personal information and videos NMAO has collected about my child;
  • request that NMAO correct or delete my child's information and videos; and
  • withdraw this consent, which will stop any further collection or use and end my child's participation.

To do any of these, I can contact my child's school or email support@nmao.us. NMAO may verify that I am the parent/guardian before acting on a request.

Acknowledgment of Participation Risk

I understand that martial-arts training and performance involve physical activity with inherent risks. My child performs and records at their own school or home under the supervision of their instructor or me, not at an NMAO facility or event. I accept responsibility for supervising my child's safe participation.

Agreement and Signature

I understand I can withdraw this consent at any time by contacting NMAO.
