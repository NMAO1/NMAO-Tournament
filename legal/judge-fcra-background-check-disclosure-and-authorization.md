# Background Check Disclosure and Authorization (Judges)

> **DRAFT — FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Starting template for counsel.
> FCRA requires the **disclosure** to appear in a document that consists *solely* of the
> disclosure (no liability waivers or extraneous text). Counsel should confirm the
> standalone-disclosure structure below, the authorization, and all state-specific
> addenda (CA, NY, MN, WA, and others impose extra notices/rights). Judges review
> videos of minors, so a background screen is required before the first assignment
> and periodically thereafter.

## Part 1 — Disclosure

NMAO Inc. ("NMAO") may obtain one or more **consumer reports** or **investigative
consumer reports** (together, a "background check") about you from a consumer
reporting agency ("CRA") for purposes of evaluating you for, and your continued
engagement as, a **volunteer/independent-contractor judge**.

The background check may include, to the extent permitted by law, searches of:
- national, state, and county **criminal records**;
- the **national and state sex-offender registries**;
- identity and address history verification.

> **NOTE TO COUNSEL:** confirm the scope above matches the CRA package NMAO selects
> (recommended: national criminal + national sex-offender registry) and applicable
> "ban-the-box"/scope limits by jurisdiction. Because judges are remote and never in
> physical contact with minors, confirm whether an investigative consumer report
> (which triggers additional notice) is in scope or should be excluded.

You will receive **"A Summary of Your Rights Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act"**
together with this disclosure. If a background check is obtained, you have the right,
upon written request within a reasonable time, to a complete and accurate disclosure
of the nature and scope of the check, and to know whether a consumer report was
requested and the name and address of the CRA that furnished it.

## Part 2 — Authorization

I have read and understand the Disclosure above and the accompanying Summary of Your
Rights Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act. I **authorize** NMAO and its designated CRA
to obtain a background check about me for the purposes described, and I authorize any
person or entity contacted by the CRA to furnish the information requested.

I understand that:
- this authorization remains valid for the duration of my engagement as a judge, and I
  authorize **periodic re-checks** (for example, annually or via continuous monitoring)
  without a new authorization, to the extent permitted by law;
- a background check is **not** a guarantee of engagement, and results are evaluated in
  accordance with applicable law;
- if NMAO intends to take adverse action based in whole or in part on a background
  check, I will first receive a **pre-adverse-action notice** with a copy of the report
  and the Summary of Rights, a reasonable opportunity to respond, and then, if
  applicable, an **adverse-action notice**.
