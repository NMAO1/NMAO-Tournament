## The key legal distinction (read first)

- **The Grand Finale prize is a SKILL CONTEST** — winners are determined by **judged
  performance**, not chance. A skill contest **may** require an entry fee. Part A
  below is its Official Rules.
- **Any random-draw giveaway (e.g., a "voter raffle") is a SWEEPSTAKES.** If it
  requires purchase/consideration to enter, it becomes an **illegal lottery**. A
  sweepstakes **must offer a free method of entry ("NO PURCHASE NECESSARY")**. Part B
  is a note + skeleton for that, to be used **only** if a raffle is enabled.

# Part B — Voter-Raffle Sweepstakes (skeleton — use ONLY if a raffle is enabled)

> A monthly **voter raffle** (random draw among people who voted) is a **sweepstakes**,
> not a skill contest. To avoid being an unlawful lottery it **must** include a free
> entry method and follow sweepstakes law. **This is a Phase-3, flag-gated feature** —
> keep it OFF until counsel approves.

Required elements (to be drafted with counsel):
- **"NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. A PURCHASE OR PAYMENT WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF
  WINNING."** A **free alternate method of entry (AMOE)** must be offered and give
  equal odds (e.g., a mail-in or free in-app entry that does not require paid
  participation).
- Eligibility (age/residency); **minors** enter via guardian; guardian receives the
  prize.
- Entry period, drawing date, **random selection**, odds statement ("odds depend on
  number of entries").
- Prize description + ARV; taxes (1099 as above); publicity release (guardian-gated).
- Sponsor, void-where-prohibited, governing law, winners list.
- **State registration/bonding** where required by prize value.
