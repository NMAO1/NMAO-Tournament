**2. Purpose & target audience.** NMAO Compete is the competitor app for the National Martial Arts Organization, a real-world martial-arts tournament league. Martial artists (any style, belt, and age; minors participate with guardian consent) enter a season-long, human-judged competition from their own dojo: submit a performance video per event, certified judges score it on a consistent rubric, and competitors earn national ratings, placements, and physical awards. Between rounds they can duel head-to-head with community voting. It brings fair, nationally-ranked, judged competition to athletes remotely, with real awards.

**3. Walkthrough / setup.** Sign in with the demo account above.
- **Compete** — competitor home: current round, per-event status, ratings. Traditional Forms shows an **"Upload"** action (the comped paid entry) — this is accessing a paid feature.
- **Duel / Arena** — watch two competitors' videos and vote; use the **Report** control on a video and **Block** a competitor (also Profile → Blocked accounts).
- **Profile** — ratings, honors, Privacy Policy, and **Account Deletion** (Profile → Delete account).

**4. External services.** Supabase (authentication, database, video/file storage, backend functions); Stripe (entry-fee payment processing via web checkout); Resend (transactional email). No third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or AI services.

**5. Regional differences.** None. Launches in the United States and functions consistently for all users — no region-specific features, content, or gating.

**6. Regulated material / authorization.** The only regulated aspect is handling of minors' data (U.S. COPPA). Every minor's account is created and consented to by a parent/guardian at onboarding (verifiable consent, media/likeness release, rules & code of conduct, privacy policy), reviewed and signed off by legal counsel. Privacy Policy in-app (Profile → Privacy Policy) and at https://school.nmao.us/privacy.html. No protected third-party material — sponsor placements are not active in this version; all content is first-party or user-generated by consented participants.

**Payments are entry into a real-world competition (Guideline 3.1.3(e)).** The only purchases are entry fees to compete in the real-world tournament (single event or season pass). Not digital content, in-app currency, or an unlockable feature — payment enters the athlete into a live, human-judged competition that culminates in physical awards (medals and a collectible medallion) shipped to the athlete. As a real-world service used outside the app, entry is handled through the organization's web checkout per Guideline 3.1.3(e). No digital-only goods, and therefore no In-App Purchase products.

**Safety / user-generated content (Guideline 1.2).** Competition videos are user-generated. Every video has a **Report** control; a competitor can **Block** another (Profile → Blocked accounts); a guardian-accepted zero-tolerance policy is agreed at onboarding; offending content and accounts are removed. Minor competitors are shown by first name + last initial only.

**Account deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v)).** Profile → Delete account permanently deletes the account and associated data.

**Distribution (Guideline 3.2).** NMAO Compete is a public consumer app. Registration is open to any martial artist in the United States — anyone can create an account from the login screen ("Create an account"). It is not limited to a specific business's employees or one organization's internal use; a competitor's school may confirm their rank, but this does not gate access.
