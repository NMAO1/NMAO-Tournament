This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
[EFFECTIVE DATE] by and between **NMAO Inc.**, the National Martial Arts
Organization ("NMAO," "Company"), and **[JUDGE FULL LEGAL NAME]** ("Judge,"
"Contractor").

## 1. Engagement & Services
NMAO engages Contractor to provide **video-based martial-arts judging services**
("Services") — reviewing assigned competitor performance videos and scoring them
against NMAO's published rubric through the NMAO judging application, on a
per-assignment basis. Contractor may **accept or decline any assignment** at their
sole discretion.

## 2. Independent Contractor Status (material term)
Contractor is an **independent contractor, not an employee, agent, partner, or joint
venturer** of NMAO. Both parties intend and acknowledge:
- Contractor **controls the manner and means** of performing the Services, sets their
  **own hours**, and uses their **own equipment** (device, internet).
- Contractor is **paid per completed assignment**, not by salary or hourly wage.
- Contractor is **not entitled to employee benefits** (health, PTO, retirement,
  workers' compensation, unemployment insurance).
- The engagement is **non-exclusive** — Contractor may perform similar services for
  others and pursue other work.
- Contractor is **responsible for all taxes** on payments (self-employment tax,
  income tax) and will receive an IRS **Form 1099** for payments totaling $600+ in a
  calendar year. NMAO will **not withhold** taxes.
- Contractor has **no authority to bind NMAO** or represent it beyond the Services.

## 3. Onboarding Gates (conditions precedent to any assignment)
No assignments are offered until Contractor has, to NMAO's satisfaction: (a) executed
this Agreement; (b) completed **Stripe Connect** payout onboarding (tax/bank info
collected by Stripe — see §5); (c) **passed a background check** (§7); and (d)
affirmed the **NMAO Integrity Creed** (§6).

## 4. Compensation
NMAO pays Contractor **$[RATE] per completed, submitted assignment** (or per pod, as
specified at assignment). Payment is disbursed via **Stripe Connect** on NMAO's
regular payout schedule. Contractor is paid only for assignments **completed and
submitted** per NMAO's standards. Rates may be updated prospectively on notice.

## 5. Payment & Tax Handling
All payouts and tax collection are handled through **Stripe Connect Express**.
**NMAO never collects or stores Contractor's raw bank account or tax-identification
data** — those go directly to Stripe. Contractor is responsible for maintaining
accurate payout and tax information in Stripe.

## 6. Impartiality, Conflicts of Interest & the Integrity Creed
Contractor affirms and will uphold the **NMAO Integrity Creed** and NMAO's
Conflict-of-Interest policy, including: scoring only what is shown, without bias or
favor; **disclosing conflicts** and **recusing** where a conflict exists (own school,
relation by blood/marriage/partnership, coaching/training the competitor within the
prior **24 months**, or any financial interest); and holding all performances and
scores in **confidence**. NMAO may reassign or void scores affected by an
undisclosed conflict.

## 7. Background Check
Contractor consents to a background check performed by a third-party consumer
reporting agency as a condition of engagement, and to periodic re-checks. NMAO may
decline or terminate engagement based on results, consistent with applicable law
(incl. FCRA and any state ban-the-box / fair-chance rules).

## 8. Confidentiality
Contractor will keep confidential all competitor information, performance videos,
scores, rubrics, and non-public NMAO materials, during and after the engagement, and
will not use them except to perform the Services.

## 9. Intellectual Property & Feedback
Scores, criterion ratings, and any feedback Contractor submits through the platform
are provided to NMAO for tournament operation and competitor development, and
Contractor grants NMAO a perpetual, royalty-free license to use them for those
purposes. NMAO's platform, rubrics, and materials remain NMAO's property.

## 10. Minor Safety & Compliance
Contractor acknowledges that competitors include **minors**, will comply with NMAO's
child-safety policies and applicable law (incl. **COPPA**), will not attempt to
contact or identify competitors outside the platform, and will report any safety
concern to NMAO.

## 11. Term & Termination
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated. **Either
party may terminate at any time, with or without cause, on [NOTICE PERIOD, e.g. 7
days'] written notice** (or immediately for breach, conflict, or failed background
check). Each assignment is a separate, discretionary engagement; nothing obligates
NMAO to offer, or Contractor to accept, any assignment. §§6, 8, 9, 12, 13 survive
termination.
