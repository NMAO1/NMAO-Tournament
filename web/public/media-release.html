NMAO TOURNAMENTS

Parental Consent & Video Release

Last updated: September 1, 2026

For: NMAO Compete (NMAO Inc.) — a human-judged martial-arts video tournament for competitors as young as 7.

This instrument is shown to the guardian during onboarding, before any information is collected from the child. Promotional/marketing use of the child's video is a separate, severable opt-in that does not gate participation.

Before you agree — what this is

You are the parent or legal guardian registering a child ("your competitor") in NMAO Compete. Because your competitor may be under 13, U.S. law (the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, "COPPA") requires your verifiable consent before we collect any information from or about your child. Please read this in full. You can decline any optional item, and you can change your mind later.

1. Who you are (identity & relationship attestation)

By agreeing, you confirm that:

  • You are at least 18 years old and are the parent or legal guardian of the competitor you are registering, with authority to consent on their behalf.
  • The information you provide about yourself — guardian full name, email, and phone (if provided) — is accurate, and we may use it to contact you and to verify your identity.
  • You understand NMAO will verify that you are the parent/guardian through a payment-card transaction you complete in your name (which sends a receipt to your account), consistent with the FTC-recognized verifiable-parental-consent method under 16 CFR §312.5(b)(2)(B), before your child participates.
  • You are consenting on behalf of the competitor named below (Section 8).

2. What we collect from and about your competitor (itemized consent to collect)

You consent to NMAO collecting the following from or about your competitor, for the purpose of running the tournament:

  • Identity & profile: first and last name, date of birth (or age bracket), declared rank (beginner / intermediate / advanced), martial-arts style, and the school they are affiliated with.
  • Competition video: the video your competitor submits for each event — this is the item that is judged. (See Section 3.)
  • Competition data generated in the app: event entries, skill ratings, placements/standings, and peer "duel" votes cast in the community-voting feature.
  • Technical/diagnostic data needed to operate and secure the app (for example, device type, app version, IP address, and usage logs), used only to run, secure, and support the Service.

We do not require your competitor's home address. We collect your contact details (Section 1) — not the child's — for communication and consent.

You understand we collect only what is reasonably necessary to run the tournament, and your child's participation is not conditioned on providing more than that.

3. The competition video (explicit consent to record, store, and display)

Video of a minor is sensitive, so this section is separate and specific.

3a. Core tournament use — required to compete. You consent to NMAO recording, uploading, storing, and displaying your competitor's competition video, and to that video being viewable by:

  • the assigned judges who score the entry (judges do not score competitors from their own school);
  • your competitor's school;
  • NMAO staff who operate and moderate the tournament;
  • you (the guardian) and the competitor; and
  • other competitors, for the in-app peer "duel" voting feature, where community members vote between entries.

You understand that:

  • Videos are hosted privately/unlisted with our storage provider (Supabase) and are not publicly searchable or discoverable.
  • Peer voting means other competitors will see your child's video within the app, and voters may see your competitor's first name and entry.
  • This core use is necessary to compete; if you do not agree to it, your competitor cannot participate.

3b. Promotional / marketing use — OPTIONAL, separate opt-in (unchecked by default).

> ☐ Optional — you may leave this unchecked and still compete.

You separately consent (only if you check this box) to NMAO using your competitor's competition video and likeness in promotional and marketing materials (for example, highlights, social or website features, sponsor-facing reels). If you do not check this box, your competitor's video will be used only to run and judge the tournament and for the peer-voting feature described in 3a — never for marketing.

You may withdraw this promotional permission at any time (Section 6); withdrawal stops future promotional use, though we may be unable to recall materials already distributed.

4. How long we keep it (retention)

  • Competition videos and competition data are retained while your account is active, plus twelve (12) months, and are deleted sooner on your request, so your family can look back on your competitor's progress over time.
  • If you opt in to the promotional use (Section 3b), videos you have allowed for that purpose may be kept on the same schedule until you withdraw that permission or request deletion.
  • Guardian contact details and limited payment records are kept as needed to operate the account and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • When retention ends, or when you request deletion, we delete the information — including from our storage provider — subject to reasonable backup-purge timelines.

5. Who else touches the data (service providers)

We use a small set of vendors, bound to protect the data and use it only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in, app hosting, and private hosting of competition videos;
  • Stripe — entry-fee payments and consent verification;
  • Email/SMS providers — account, deadline, and results notifications.

We do not sell your competitor's information, and we do not use it for behavioral advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. Your rights as the parent/guardian (review, delete, withdraw)

At any time, you may:

  • Review the personal information and videos we have collected about your competitor;
  • Correct or delete that information and those videos;
  • Refuse to permit further collection or use, and withdraw this consent — which ends your competitor's participation.

To do any of these, use the in-app account controls or email support@nmao.us. We will verify that you are the parent/guardian before acting, and we will respond within a reasonable time (typically within 30 days). Withdrawing consent or deleting data will not affect the lawfulness of processing before your request.

7. How your consent is verified (VPC method)

Your consent is verified using a payment-card transaction you complete in your name, an FTC-recognized verifiable-parental-consent method under 16 CFR §312.5(b)(2)(B). The transaction sends a notice to your account confirming that the person granting these permissions is you. Checking the boxes below records the specific permissions you are granting; the card transaction confirms your identity as the parent/guardian.

8. The permissions you are agreeing to (checkboxes)

  • ☐ I confirm I am the parent/legal guardian of the named competitor and that my information is accurate. (Section 1)
  • ☐ I consent to NMAO collecting the competitor information listed in Section 2 to run the tournament.
  • ☐ I consent to my competitor's competition video being recorded, stored, and shown for judging, community/peer voting, my competitor's school, and NMAO staff, as described in Section 3a. (Required to compete.)
  • ☐ (Optional) I also consent to NMAO using my competitor's video and likeness for promotional/marketing purposes, as described in Section 3b. (Not required to compete.)
  • ☐ I have read and agree to the competition rules and code of conduct, and understand NMAO has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior — such content and the responsible accounts are removed.
  • ☐ I have read and agree to the [Privacy Policy](https://school.nmao.us/privacy.html).
