# Judge Creed and Code of Conduct

> This code governs conduct; counsel should confirm it aligns with the Independent
> Contractor Agreement (no language that undercuts contractor status), the child-safety
> obligations, and any mandatory-reporting framing for the target jurisdictions.

As an NMAO judge, I review the performances of martial-arts competitors — many of whom
are **minors** — and I hold a position of trust. I agree to uphold this Creed and Code
of Conduct each time I score.

## 1. Integrity and impartiality
- I score solely on the merits of the performance against the published rubric.
- I do **not** favor or penalize a competitor based on their school, name, appearance,
  race, sex, disability, religion, or any characteristic unrelated to the performance.
- I do not accept anything of value in exchange for a score, and I do not attempt to
  influence, or let others influence, my scoring.

## 2. Conflicts of interest and recusal
- I will **recuse** myself from any assignment involving a competitor I coach, teach,
  am related to, or with whom I have a close personal or financial relationship.
- I will disclose to NMAO any circumstance that a reasonable person would view as a
  conflict of interest.

## 3. Child safety and professional boundaries
- I treat every competitor with respect and professionalism.
- I will **not** attempt to identify, contact, or communicate with any competitor or
  their family through the platform or outside it.
- If I encounter content that suggests a child is in danger, is being abused, or is
  otherwise at risk, I will **immediately report it to NMAO** through the designated
  channel and will not investigate or intervene myself.

## 4. Confidentiality and handling of minors' media
- Competitor videos and personal data are **confidential**. I will access them only to
  perform an assignment, only through the NMAO platform.
- I will **not** download, screen-record, screenshot, copy, retain, publish, or share
  any competitor video or personal information, or use it for any purpose other than
  scoring the assignment.
- I will review assignments in a private setting where others cannot view the content.

## 5. Account and platform security
- I will keep my judge account credentials secure and will not share my account.
- I will review assignments on a device and network I reasonably believe to be secure,
  and I will sign out on shared devices.

## 6. Timeliness and communication
- I will complete accepted assignments within the stated deadline or promptly recuse so
  they can be reassigned.
- I will communicate with NMAO honestly and professionally.

## 7. Consequences
- I understand that violating this Code — and especially any breach of child-safety or
  confidentiality obligations — may result in immediate removal from judging, forfeiture
  of pending pay, and, where warranted, referral to appropriate authorities, in addition
  to any remedies in my Independent Contractor Agreement and applicable law.
