Competition Rules & Code of Conduct

Last updated: August 22, 2026

These rules explain how NMAO video tournaments work and the conduct we expect from everyone who takes part — competitors, parents/guardians, schools, and judges. By entering a tournament (or, for a minor, by a parent/guardian entering on the competitor's behalf), you agree to follow these rules. They cover how you compete fairly and how you treat others. They are separate from, and in addition to, our Privacy Policy.

Zero tolerance. NMAO has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior. Content that violates these rules, and the accounts responsible for it, are removed.

Eligibility & Accounts

  • Competitors may be as young as 7. A parent or legal guardian must create and manage the account for any competitor under 18 and is responsible for the competitor's participation.
  • Provide accurate information — real name, correct date of birth, and honest rank. Competitors are placed into age and skill divisions based on this information; misstating it is a fairness violation.
  • One account per competitor. Do not create duplicate or fake accounts.
  • Your school may set or confirm your rank and enable certain features.

How Competition Works

  • You enter an event by submitting a short video of your performance. Entries are grouped into fair divisions and pods by age, rank, and rating.
  • Certified human judges score entries. Judges do not score competitors from their own school.
  • Results produce placements, ratings, and season standings across the season's tournaments. Placing competitors receive physical medals, shipped to the competitor's school for the instructor to distribute.

Video & Entry Rules

  • Submit your own current performance. Do not submit someone else's video, a reused or old video misrepresented as new, or a video that has been edited to misrepresent the performance.
  • Keep the camera steady and the competitor clearly visible, as described in the app's guidance.
  • Videos must show only the martial-arts performance. Do not include other people without their consent, personal contact information, or anything that violates the Code of Conduct below.
  • Perform only techniques you can do safely and within your ability, in a safe space. We recommend adult supervision, especially for younger competitors and weapons events.

Fair Play & Integrity

  • No cheating, impersonation, or misrepresenting your identity, age, or rank.
  • Do not attempt to manipulate judging, ratings, votes, or results — including coordinating votes, using multiple accounts, or pressuring others.
  • Compete honestly and accept results in good sportsmanship.

Community Duels & Voting

“Duels” are an optional feature where two competitors' short videos are shown head-to-head and other competitors vote on which they prefer. If you take part in duels or vote:

  • Watch the videos and vote honestly on the performance — not based on who someone is or what school they're from.
  • Do not organize or coordinate voting to unfairly influence outcomes.
  • Everything in the Code of Conduct applies to duels and voting.

Code of Conduct — Zero Tolerance

Treat everyone with respect. The following are not tolerated anywhere in the Service — in videos, names, messages, or any interaction:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or intimidation of any person;
  • Hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar characteristics;
  • Sexually explicit, violent, graphic, or otherwise objectionable content;
  • Content that endangers or exploits a minor;
  • Impersonation, doxxing, or sharing others' private information;
  • Spam, scams, or attempts to defeat safety features.

Objectionable content and abusive users are removed. Because minors use this Service, we enforce this strictly.

Reporting & Blocking

If you see content or behavior that breaks these rules, use the in-app report option — reported content is hidden from voting immediately and reviewed by NMAO staff. You can also block another competitor so you no longer see their content and they no longer see yours. You can reach us any time at support@nmao.us.

Enforcement

Depending on the severity, a violation may result in removal of the content, disqualification of an entry or result, a warning, suspension, or termination of the account. We may act without prior notice where needed to protect the community, especially minors. Entry fees are not refunded for entries removed or disqualified due to a rules violation.

Payments, Refunds & Cancellation

Entering a tournament requires an entry credit — purchased as a single entry, a monthly plan, or a season pass, and spent one credit per event you enter. Payment is handled by our payment provider (Stripe) in your device browser.

Refunds on credits

  • Unused credits are refundable within 14 days of purchase — email support@nmao.us from your account email.
  • A credit is “used” the moment it is spent to enter an event (an entry is created for a round). Used credits are non-refundable, because entry into a real, human-judged competition has been provided.
  • Season pass: if you request within 14 days, we refund the pass at the per-credit rate for the credits you have not yet used.

Monthly plan (subscription)

  • The monthly plan renews automatically each tournament month and you can cancel anytime — cancellation stops all future charges.
  • When you cancel, you keep the plan and any credit already granted through the end of the current paid month. We do not refund partial months, and unused monthly credits roll over while your plan is active.

Other

  • Entries removed or disqualified for a rules violation are not refunded.
  • We promptly refund genuine billing errors or duplicate charges — just contact us.

Changes & Contact

We may update these rules as the tournament evolves; material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above. Questions about these rules or the Code of Conduct: support@nmao.us.
