Description of the Service

NMAO Compete is an iOS application that supports a real-world, human-judged martial-arts video tournament. In general terms, the Service allows:

  • A parent or legal guardian to register a competitor (who may be a minor) and manage that competitor's participation;
  • Competitors to record and submit a short martial-arts performance video as a tournament entry;
  • Certified human judges to review and score submitted entries;
  • Results to be ranked and organized into a season of approximately nine (9) tournaments;
  • Competitors to participate in peer "duels," in which two entry videos are presented side by side and other competitors vote; and
  • Placing competitors to receive physical, shipped medals and related recognition.

The Service also facilitates tournament registration payments (through an external payment processor), optional school/dues billing, and a sponsor "Store" that links to third-party sponsor websites. NMAO may add, modify, or discontinue features at any time. The tournament is a real-world competition; nothing in the Service guarantees any particular placement, award, rating, or outcome.
