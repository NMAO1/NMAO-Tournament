# NMAO — Badge Catalog & System

*The full badge collection. Every badge is **unique** (its own emblem, name, and
earn rule), designed to work as a digital collectible **and** a physical **pin or
patch** (e-commerce + sold at live Grand Finales). Expands `competitor-growth-and-
badges.md` §6.*

Last updated: 2026-08-08

---

## Design principles

- **One emblem per badge.** Every badge has a distinct, iconic silhouette (no
  reused icons) so it reads instantly at pin size and is worth collecting.
- **Pin/patch-ready from day one.** Bold silhouette, ≤4 enamel colors + metal,
  legible at ~1–1.25", die-struck soft/hard-enamel pin or embroidered patch.
- **Rarity = finish** (digital *and* physical), so a rarer badge is a rarer pin:
  | Rarity | Digital finish | Physical pin |
  |---|---|---|
  | Common | antique bronze | bronze plating |
  | Uncommon | brushed silver | silver plating |
  | Rare | polished gold | gold plating |
  | Epic | metallic **spectrum** enamel | spectrum hard-enamel |
  | Legendary | black-nickel / **platinum** + gem accent | limited-edition, numbered |
- **Tiered badges** (Bronze/Silver/Gold levels) map to the plating ladder.
- **Locked state teases the collection.** A locked badge shows as a **dark embossed
  silhouette** — you can see the *shape* of what's earnable (drives the chase).
  **Hidden** badges show only a **"?"** until unlocked.
- **The reveal is a moment.** On earn: a light sweep crosses the silhouette, the
  emblem **ignites into its finish** (fills with color/metal), a small particle
  burst + haptic + chime, a "Badge earned" toast, then it settles into the grid in
  full color. (Reanimated/Skia in the app; the web mock hints at it with a shimmer.)

## Badges page — UI behavior

- **Grid** of all badges; earned = full-color metallic finish, locked = dark
  embossed silhouette (shape visible), hidden = "?".
- **Filters:** category, rarity, earned/locked, "close to earning" (progress bars on
  tiered/streak badges).
- **Detail sheet:** big emblem, story, exact earn rule + progress, date earned,
  rarity/finish, and a **"Get the pin"** link (e-commerce) for earned badges.
- **Earn reveal:** light-sweep → ignite → particle burst → haptic → toast, then it
  locks into the grid. Replayable from the detail sheet.

## Physical product notes (pins / patches)

- Each emblem ships as a **die-struck enamel pin** and an **embroidered patch**.
- **Rarity = plating** (bronze/silver/gold), Epic = spectrum hard-enamel, Legendary
  = numbered limited editions (black-nickel/platinum + a gem).
- **Sell as:** singles, **category sets** (e.g. the six Mastery pins), and the
  annual **Gem pin** — a yearly collectible color-matched to that season's medal.
  Ideal Grand-Finale merch and an e-commerce anchor.
- Earning a badge digitally unlocks its pin in the store (and could include one free
  pin with a placement medal shipment).

## Data

`badges (code, name, emblem_key, category, rarity, tiered, earn_rule, hidden,
sku)` + `badge_awards (competitor_id, badge_code, tier, round_id?, awarded_at,
seen)` — `seen=false` triggers the reveal animation on next open. `sku` links to the
e-commerce pin/patch.
